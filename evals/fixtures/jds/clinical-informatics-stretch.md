# Clinical Informatics Analyst II -- Epic Inpatient and Reporting

**Company:** Sorrel Valley Health System
**Team:** Information Services, Clinical Applications
**Location:** Sorrel Valley, PA (remote within PA, OH, or NY after training)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Sorrel Valley Health System

Sorrel Valley Health System is a regional academic health system with five
hospitals and a growing ambulatory network. Our Clinical Applications team
builds and supports Epic for 14,000 clinicians and owns the reporting that
leadership uses to track quality and safety.

## About the role

We are hiring an analyst who will build Epic inpatient clinical content and
own the data behind it. Half of the role is Epic ClinDoc build and support.
The other half is reporting and integration: writing SQL against the Clarity
reporting database, building Tableau dashboards for nursing quality, and
testing HL7 interfaces with device and lab vendors. Clinical experience is a
strong plus, but this is an analyst role, not a nursing role.

## Responsibilities

- Build and maintain Epic ClinDoc content: flowsheets, navigators, care plans, and patient lists
- Translate clinical workflow requests into build specifications and test scripts
- Write SQL queries against Clarity to answer quality, safety, and operational questions
- Build and maintain Tableau dashboards for nursing quality indicators and clinical decision support performance
- Test and troubleshoot HL7 interfaces for bedside devices, lab results, and admission messages
- Run Epic Reporting Workbench reports for end users and retire reports that duplicate dashboards
- Support upgrades and go-live events, including after-hours cutover testing
- Take part in the on-call rotation for Epic inpatient applications (one week in six)

## Required qualifications

- Epic ClinDoc or Inpatient Orders certification, or completion within 90 days of hire (sponsored)
- Three or more years of experience as an EHR analyst or in a technical clinical informatics role
- Proficiency in SQL, including joins, subqueries, and window functions
- Experience building Tableau dashboards for clinical or operational audiences
- Working knowledge of HL7 v2 message structure and interface testing
- Experience writing test scripts and validating EHR build

## Preferred qualifications

- Clinical license (RN, pharmacist, or respiratory therapist)
- Epic Clarity data model certification
- Experience with clinical decision support build and alert performance analysis
- Python for automating reporting tasks

## Our team

- Weekly build reviews and change control for every production change
- A training budget for Epic certifications and conferences
