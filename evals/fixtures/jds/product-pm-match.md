# Product Manager, Supplier Experience

**Company:** Umbrella Analytics
**Team:** Marketplace Product
**Location:** Chicago, IL (hybrid, two days in the office)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Umbrella Analytics

Umbrella Analytics runs a procurement marketplace that connects mid-sized
manufacturers with vetted suppliers. Our product teams are small and
cross-functional, and we plan work around what we learn from customers rather
than around feature lists.

## About the role

We are hiring a Product Manager for the supplier experience: the tools
suppliers use to join the marketplace, manage their catalog, and respond to
requests for quotes. You will own the roadmap for this area, partner with a
designer and two engineering squads, and be the main voice of suppliers in
the company.

## What you will do

- Own the product roadmap for supplier onboarding, catalog management, and quoting
- Run customer interviews and usability sessions, and turn what you learn into priorities
- Write clear product requirement documents and user stories for engineering
- Prioritize the backlog with a transparent framework and explain the trade-offs
- Partner with design on research, prototypes, and usability testing
- Work with sales, support, and finance stakeholders to plan releases and communicate changes
- Define success metrics for each release and review them after launch
- Run beta programs with a small group of suppliers before wider launches

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Two or more years of product management, associate product management, or a closely related role
- Experience running customer interviews and synthesizing user research
- A track record of writing product requirement documents that engineers can build from
- Strong prioritization and stakeholder communication skills
- Experience working day to day with designers and engineers

### Preferred

- A design or research background
- Experience with B2B marketplaces, onboarding flows, or workflow software
- Comfort using spreadsheets or dashboards to track adoption
- Experience managing beta programs and release notes

## How we work

- Weekly planning and a monthly roadmap review open to the whole company
- Customer conversations are part of every sprint
- Short written documents over long meetings
- Product managers and designers share ownership of discovery

## Benefits

- Competitive salary and annual bonus
- Hybrid schedule with a commuter benefit
- Learning budget and a mentorship program
- Generous parental leave and flexible time off

## Interview process

1. A conversation with the hiring manager
2. A discussion of a product you shipped, from discovery to launch
3. A written exercise: turn customer notes into a one-page proposal
4. Conversations with a designer, an engineer, and a stakeholder from sales
