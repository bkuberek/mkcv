# UX Engineer, Design Systems

**Company:** Pixelwright Labs
**Team:** Design Systems
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Pixelwright Labs

Pixelwright Labs builds scheduling and payments software for independent
fitness studios. Our web app and our studio-branded mobile apps share one
design system, maintained by a team of two designers and three UX engineers.

## About the role

You will build our design system in code. You will turn Figma components into
production React components written in TypeScript, document them in
Storybook, and keep design tokens in sync between Figma and code. You will
own the quality bar for accessibility in every component and help product
teams adopt the system.

## What you will do

- Build and maintain accessible React components in TypeScript for our component library
- Document components, usage guidelines, and examples in Storybook
- Maintain the pipeline that exports design tokens from Figma variables to CSS custom properties and our mobile themes
- Write semantic HTML and modern CSS, including layout, theming, and motion
- Test components for accessibility with screen readers and automated checks
- Review pull requests from product teams that use or extend the system
- Pair with designers on new components and on how they behave in code
- Measure adoption of design systems components across the codebase

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Four or more years of front-end development experience
- Production experience with React and TypeScript
- Experience building a component library and documenting it in Storybook
- Expert HTML and CSS skills
- Strong accessibility knowledge, including WCAG 2.1 AA and ARIA patterns
- Fluency in Figma and experience working closely with designers
- Experience with design tokens across platforms

### Preferred

- A background in visual or product design
- Experience with visual regression testing
- Experience with React Native or another mobile framework
- Contributions to a public design system

## How we work

- The design systems team treats product teams as customers
- Every component ships with documentation, tests, and an accessibility review
- We release the component library every two weeks

## Benefits

- Health, dental, and vision coverage
- Free studio memberships through our customers
- Home office stipend
- Paid parental leave

## Interview process

1. Conversation with the design systems lead
2. Technical interview: build a small accessible component in React
