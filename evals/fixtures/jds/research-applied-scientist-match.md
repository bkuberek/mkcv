# Applied Scientist, Weather and Energy Forecasting

**Company:** Zephyrine Energy
**Team:** Forecasting Science
**Location:** Kestrel Bay, WA (hybrid) or remote in the US; visa sponsorship available
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Zephyrine Energy

Zephyrine Energy forecasts wind and solar generation and electricity prices
for utilities, grid operators, and energy traders across North America and
Europe. Better forecasts let more renewable energy onto the grid with less
backup generation. Our forecasting science team of 14 includes atmospheric
scientists, statisticians, and machine learning researchers.

## About the role

You will research, build, and ship deep learning models that turn weather
model output, satellite data, and plant telemetry into probabilistic forecasts
of renewable generation. You will own models from the first experiment to
production, working with our ML engineers who run the serving platform. Many
of our scientists come from academia; we will help you learn the production
side.

## What you will do

- Develop deep learning models in PyTorch for short-term and day-ahead solar and wind forecasting
- Build probabilistic forecasting methods and evaluate them with proper scoring rules such as CRPS
- Work with time series and gridded weather data at scale
- Explore graph neural networks and other architectures that capture spatial relationships between sites
- Design rigorous experiments and backtests, and communicate results to customers and colleagues
- Partner with ML engineers to move models into production and monitor their performance
- Keep up with research in weather and climate machine learning and bring useful ideas into the product

## What we are looking for

### Required

- PhD or equivalent research experience in atmospheric science, physics, statistics, computer science, or a related field
- Three or more years of hands-on deep learning research with PyTorch or JAX
- Strong Python skills and experience with scientific computing libraries such as NumPy and xarray
- Experience with probabilistic forecasting or uncertainty quantification
- Experience training models on GPUs with large datasets
- A record of publications or other evidence of independent research
- Clear communication with technical and non-technical audiences

### Preferred

- Experience with numerical weather prediction data
- Experience in renewable energy or power systems forecasting
- Experience deploying models with Kubernetes or processing data with Apache Spark
- Experience working with industry partners or customers

## How we work

- Scientists own their models in production, with support from ML engineers
- Weekly research reviews where we share failures as well as wins
- Time set aside each quarter for exploratory research
