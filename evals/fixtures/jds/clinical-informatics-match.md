# Clinical Informatics Specialist, Nursing

**Company:** Lakemont Health Partners
**Team:** Clinical Informatics
**Location:** Lakemont, OH (hybrid; on site for go-live and unit rounding)
**Employment type:** Full time, exempt
**Pay range:** $92,000 to $118,000 per year, based on experience

## About Lakemont Health Partners

Lakemont Health Partners is a nonprofit health system with three hospitals
(610 beds in total) and 40 outpatient practices. We moved to Epic in 2020 and
are now focused on optimization: fewer clicks for clinicians, safer care, and
documentation that supports quality reporting. Our nursing informatics team
works for the Chief Nursing Information Officer.

## About the role

As a Clinical Informatics Specialist you will be the bridge between bedside
nurses and the Epic analyst team. You will find where documentation slows
nurses down, design better workflows, and make sure every change is tested,
taught, and measured. You will lead the nursing side of upgrades and new
functionality, including go-live support.

## What you will do

- Partner with nursing leaders to analyze current workflows and design future-state workflows in Epic ClinDoc and Inpatient Orders
- Write clinical requirements and test scripts, and validate the analyst team's build before release
- Lead nursing readiness for Epic upgrades and new functionality: training plans, tip sheets, and go-live command center support
- Run the nursing super-user program across all three hospitals
- Review clinical decision support for nursing, including alert criteria, override data, and alert fatigue
- Measure the impact of optimization work on documentation time and quality indicators, and report results to nursing leadership
- Respond to escalated nursing tickets and identify patterns that need a workflow fix rather than more training
- Serve on the nursing informatics council and the clinical decision support committee

## Qualifications

### Required

- Active RN license in Ohio or a compact state
- BSN
- At least five years of clinical nursing experience, acute care preferred
- At least two years of experience in nursing informatics, EHR optimization, or as an Epic super-user with project responsibilities
- Hands-on experience with Epic inpatient nursing documentation
- Experience leading or supporting an EHR go-live
- Strong teaching and presentation skills with adult learners

### Preferred

- MSN in nursing informatics, completed or in progress
- ANCC Informatics Nursing Certification (NI-BC)
- Critical care experience
- Experience with Epic reporting tools to measure workflow changes
- Bilingual in English and Spanish

## What success looks like in the first year

- You have led at least one nursing workflow optimization from analysis to measured results
- Super-users at all three hospitals know you and bring problems to you first
- Nursing leaders trust your release notes and training plans
