# Enterprise Account Executive -- New Business

**Company:** Vantorra Cloud
**Team:** Enterprise Sales, North America
**Location:** Remote (US Northeast)
**Employment type:** Full time
**On-target earnings:** $280,000 to $320,000 (50/50 base and variable), uncapped commission

## About Vantorra Cloud

Vantorra Cloud sells spend management and procurement automation software to
companies with more than 2,000 employees. We doubled ARR last year and we are
hiring hunters to open new enterprise territories.

## About the role

This is a quota-carrying, new logo role. You will own full-cycle sales in a
named-account territory, from prospecting and cold outreach to negotiation
and close. You will build your own pipeline, run disciplined MEDDICC
qualification, and close six-figure deals with CFOs and procurement leaders.
Top performers here make President's Club in their first year.

## What you will do

- Own a $1.4M annual quota for new logo ARR in a territory of 150 named enterprise accounts
- Generate pipeline through cold calling, email sequences, LinkedIn, and events, targeting 4x pipeline coverage
- Qualify every opportunity with MEDDICC and keep Salesforce forecasting accurate to within 10%
- Run discovery, build business cases, and lead demos with solutions engineers
- Lead negotiation with procurement and legal through contract signature
- Sell to executive stakeholders including CFOs, CPOs, and VPs of finance
- Review your calls in Gong with your manager every week
- Hand off closed accounts to customer success and stay involved in early expansion

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Five or more years of closing experience in B2B SaaS, at least three selling new logo deals to enterprise accounts
- A record of quota attainment above 100% in at least three of the last four years
- Experience with full-cycle sales and average deal sizes above $150,000
- Proficiency with MEDDICC, MEDDPICC, or a similar qualification method
- Daily use of Salesforce for pipeline management and forecasting
- Experience with Gong or another conversation intelligence tool
- Strong negotiation skills with procurement teams

### Preferred

- Experience selling procurement, finance, or spend management software
- President's Club recognition
- Experience building a territory from scratch
- Background in customer success or account management with expansion results

## How we sell

- Weekly pipeline reviews and deal strategy sessions
- Mutual action plans for every deal over $100,000
- Solutions engineers, sales development representatives, and deal desk support for every account executive
