# Marketing Operations Specialist

**Company:** Quorvex Software
**Team:** Revenue Marketing
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Quorvex Software

Quorvex Software makes field inspection software for construction and
facilities teams. We sell to mid-market and enterprise companies through a
sales-led motion, and marketing is responsible for 60% of our pipeline. Our
marketing team of 18 runs paid, content, events, and lifecycle programs.

## About the role

The Marketing Operations Specialist keeps our marketing systems and data
healthy. You will build and QA email programs in Marketo, manage the sync
between Marketo and Salesforce, maintain lead scoring and routing, and report
on how campaigns turn into pipeline. This role is ideal for someone with two
to four years in marketing operations who loves systems and clean data.

## What you will do

- Build, test, and launch email campaigns and nurture programs in Marketo
- Maintain the Marketo and Salesforce integration, campaign member statuses, and lead routing rules
- Own lead scoring models with sales leadership and update them every quarter
- Build multi-touch attribution reports that connect campaigns to pipeline and revenue
- Write SQL queries against our data warehouse to answer funnel questions
- Manage UTM standards and Google Analytics 4 tracking for the website
- Keep the database clean: deduplication, enrichment, and data hygiene audits
- Support the HubSpot instance our partner marketing team still uses
- Produce weekly funnel dashboards in Excel and Google Sheets until our BI rollout is complete

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Two to four years in marketing operations or revenue operations at a B2B company
- Hands-on Marketo experience, ideally Marketo Certified Expert
- Strong Salesforce skills, including campaigns, reports, and lead management
- Experience designing and maintaining lead scoring and lead routing
- Working SQL skills
- Experience with attribution reporting
- High attention to detail and a habit of documenting processes

### Preferred

- HubSpot experience
- Experience with Zapier or another integration tool
- Advanced Excel skills, including pivot tables and lookups
- Experience with email deliverability and compliance rules
- Experience in a construction technology or B2B SaaS company

## How we work

- Marketing and sales share one funnel and one set of definitions
- Every campaign launches with a QA checklist and tracking plan
- We document every process in our team wiki

## Benefits

- Health, dental, and vision insurance
- 401(k) with match
- Remote work stipend
- Learning budget for Marketo and Salesforce certifications

1. Recruiter screen
2. Interview with the marketing operations manager
