# Senior Platform Engineer

**Company:** Acme Corp
**Team:** Platform Engineering
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Acme Corp

Acme Corp builds scheduling and dispatch software for field service
companies. Our customers run more than 60 internal and customer-facing
services on a Kubernetes platform that the Platform Engineering team builds
and operates. We value ownership, blameless postmortems, and pragmatic
engineering over novelty.

## About the role

You will join a team of eight engineers that runs the container platform,
the shared data stores, and the tooling that other engineering teams use to
ship. This is a hands-on senior role. You will design improvements, write
the code, and share the pager with the rest of the team.

## What you will do

- Run and improve our Kubernetes clusters, including upgrades, capacity planning, and cost
- Write platform services and command-line tooling in Go
- Operate our PostgreSQL databases: replication, backups, schema migrations, and query tuning
- Own observability standards: metrics, tracing, logging, dashboards, and alert quality
- Take part in incident response, run postmortems, and turn the findings into fixes
- Provision cloud infrastructure with Terraform and review changes from other teams
- Write design documents and mentor engineers on production readiness

## What we are looking for

### Required

- At least five years of professional software or infrastructure engineering
- Strong experience running production workloads on Kubernetes
- Professional experience writing Go
- Hands-on experience operating PostgreSQL in production
- Practical observability experience with metrics and distributed tracing
- Experience with incident response and an on-call rotation

### Preferred

- Experience with Terraform or a similar infrastructure as code tool
- A record of reducing cloud spend or deployment time
- Experience with OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, and Grafana
- Comfort writing runbooks and leading blameless postmortems

## How we work

- Small, reversible changes with measurable goals
- Written design documents for anything that affects more than one team
- A shared on-call rotation with a hard cap on pages per week
- Time reserved every sprint for reliability work

## Benefits

- Competitive salary and equity
- Remote-first with a yearly in-person team week
- Home office stipend and learning budget
- Generous parental leave and flexible time off

## Interview process

1. A 30-minute conversation with the hiring manager
2. A technical discussion about a system you have operated
3. A design exercise on a platform problem
4. Conversations with two future teammates
