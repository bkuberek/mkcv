# Junior Data Scientist, Customer Analytics

**Company:** Umbrella Analytics
**Team:** Customer Analytics
**Location:** Boston, MA (hybrid)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Umbrella Analytics

Umbrella Analytics runs a procurement marketplace for mid-sized
manufacturers. The Customer Analytics team turns marketplace data into
decisions for product, sales, and operations teams.

## About the role

We are hiring a junior data scientist who enjoys turning questions into
analyses and analyses into clear recommendations. You will work with product
and operations partners, build dashboards that people actually use, and run
the statistical analyses behind our customer research. You will learn from a
senior data scientist and a team that reviews each other's work.

## What you will do

- Answer business questions with analyses in Python and SQL
- Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau for product and operations teams
- Run regression and hypothesis tests to understand customer behavior
- Clean and join data from several source systems and document the definitions
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders in plain language
- Review colleagues' queries and notebooks and share your own for review
- Help evaluate simple forecasting and classification models

## What we are looking for

### Required

- A degree in statistics, mathematics, computer science, economics, or a related field
- Experience with Python (pandas) and SQL
- Solid understanding of statistics: regression, hypothesis testing, and confidence intervals
- Experience building dashboards or reports in Tableau or a similar tool
- Clear written and verbal communication

### Preferred

- Internship or project experience with real business data
- Experience with R or scikit-learn
- Experience with forecasting or time-series analysis
- Experience teaching or tutoring that shows you can explain ideas simply

## How we work

- Analyses are reviewed by a peer before they are shared
- Assumptions and data definitions are written down
- Stakeholders are involved early, not only at the end
- Learning time is part of the job

## Benefits

- Competitive salary and annual bonus
- Hybrid schedule with a commuter benefit
- Learning budget and a mentorship program
- Generous parental leave and flexible time off

## Interview process

1. A conversation with the hiring manager
2. A walkthrough of a data project you completed
3. A take-home analysis on a small dataset, about two hours
4. Conversations with two analysts and a product partner
