# Senior Customer Success Manager, Enterprise

**Company:** Plumline Software
**Team:** Customer Success
**Location:** Remote (US East Coast) or hybrid in Quarry Point, NJ
**Employment type:** Full time
**Compensation:** $120,000 to $140,000 base plus variable tied to retention

## About Plumline Software

Plumline Software makes contract lifecycle and vendor management software for
legal and procurement teams. More than 700 companies use Plumline to draft,
approve, and track their contracts. We are a 400-person SaaS company with
$110M in ARR, and most of our growth now comes from existing customers.

## About the role

You will own a book of enterprise accounts and be responsible for their
renewals, adoption, and growth. You will be the customer's trusted advisor,
from onboarding through each renewal, and you will work with account
executives on expansion opportunities. We measure this role on gross and net
revenue retention.

## What you will do

- Own a book of 25 to 35 enterprise accounts, about $6M to $8M in ARR
- Manage renewals end to end, including forecasting in Salesforce and negotiating with customer procurement teams
- Lead onboarding for new enterprise customers and get them to first value quickly
- Run QBRs and executive business reviews with customer executives
- Monitor account health in Gainsight and act early on churn risk
- Identify expansion opportunities and partner with account executives to close them
- Represent the customer's voice to product, including through our customer advisory board
- Mentor newer customer success managers

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Five or more years in customer success or account management at a B2B SaaS company
- Ownership of renewals for an enterprise book of business
- A record of strong net revenue retention and reducing churn
- Experience running QBRs with director and VP-level stakeholders
- Proficiency with Salesforce and Gainsight or a similar customer success platform
- Excellent written and verbal communication

### Preferred

- Experience selling to or working with procurement or legal teams
- Comfort with SQL or BI tools for analyzing product usage data
- Experience building health scores or churn playbooks
- Experience mentoring customer success managers

## How we work

- Customer success owns renewals; account executives own new business and large expansions
- Health reviews every week, renewal forecast every month
- Customers' feedback goes straight to product managers through a shared board
