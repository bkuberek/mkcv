# Senior eLearning Developer

**Company:** Quillbrook Learning
**Team:** Custom Learning Studio
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Quillbrook Learning

Quillbrook Learning is a custom e-learning studio. We build simulations,
compliance programs, and product training for pharmaceutical, banking, and
software clients, and we are known for e-learning that does not look like
e-learning. Our studio has 40 designers, developers, and producers.

## About the role

You will be a senior developer on client projects, turning approved
storyboards into polished, highly interactive courses. You will own the
technical side of our builds: advanced Storyline development with JavaScript,
Adobe Captivate for clients who require it, xAPI tracking to a learning record
store, and motion graphics in After Effects. You will also mentor two junior
developers and set our development standards.

## What you will do

- Build complex interactions and branching scenarios in Articulate Storyline, extended with JavaScript
- Develop responsive courses in Adobe Captivate for clients on that platform
- Implement xAPI statements and test tracking against client learning record stores
- Publish SCORM packages and troubleshoot LMS issues with client administrators
- Produce motion graphics and animated explainers in After Effects
- Review storyboards for technical feasibility before development starts
- Make every course meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility requirements
- Mentor junior developers and run code and build reviews

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Five or more years of professional e-learning development
- Expert-level Articulate Storyline, including JavaScript triggers and custom variables
- Production experience with Adobe Captivate
- Hands-on xAPI implementation and learning record store testing
- Motion graphics experience in After Effects
- Experience publishing and troubleshooting SCORM in several LMS platforms
- A portfolio of advanced interactions and simulations

### Preferred

- Experience in a custom e-learning agency or studio
- HTML and CSS for custom course templates
- Experience mentoring other developers
- Background in instructional design or teaching

## How we work

- Every project has an instructional designer, a developer, and a producer
- Client reviews happen in our review tool with two rounds per milestone
- We share build techniques in a weekly studio show-and-tell

## Benefits

- Health, dental, and vision coverage
- 401(k) with a 4% match
- A hardware and software stipend
- Flexible hours across US time zones

## Interview process

1. Portfolio review with the studio director
2. A technical conversation about one of your builds
3. A paid two-hour build exercise in Storyline
