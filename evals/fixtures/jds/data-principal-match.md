# Principal Data Engineer, Data Platform

**Company:** Sablecrest Financial
**Team:** Data Platform
**Location:** Emberton, IL (hybrid) or remote in the US
**Employment type:** Full time
**Base salary:** $230,000 to $265,000 plus bonus and long-term incentives

## About Sablecrest Financial

Sablecrest Financial provides consumer lending and card products to 3 million
customers. Data is central to how we underwrite, detect fraud, and serve
customers, and it is also heavily regulated. Our data platform organization of
45 engineers builds the lakehouse, streaming systems, and governance tools
that every team depends on.

## About the role

As Principal Data Engineer you will set the technical direction for the data
platform. You will lead architecture across several teams, design the systems
that move us from nightly batch to streaming, and make data governance part of
the platform rather than an afterthought. You will not manage people, but you
will grow them: mentoring senior and staff engineers is a core part of the job.

## What you will do

- Define the target architecture for our lakehouse on AWS and lead its delivery across four teams
- Design streaming pipelines on Kafka for fraud, payments, and customer events
- Set standards for batch and streaming processing with Spark
- Lead the rollout of data contracts between application teams and the platform
- Build data governance into the platform: access controls, masking of personal data, lineage, and audit evidence
- Set direction for orchestration with Airflow and transformation with dbt
- Drive cost efficiency and reliability targets for platform workloads
- Write and review design documents and chair architecture reviews
- Mentor senior and staff engineers and help shape our engineering career ladder

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Twelve or more years in data engineering, including at least three at the staff or principal level
- Deep experience with Spark and Kafka at large scale
- Experience designing and running a lakehouse or large cloud data warehouse
- Production experience with Airflow and dbt
- Experience leading a large migration from a legacy warehouse
- A track record of technical leadership across multiple teams without direct authority
- Experience with data governance in a regulated industry

### Preferred

- Experience with Snowflake or Apache Iceberg
- Experience with Apache Flink for stateful stream processing
- Financial services or insurance industry experience
- Public speaking or writing about data engineering

## How we work

- Platform teams have product managers and treat internal teams as customers
- We track cost, freshness, and quality as first-class platform metrics
