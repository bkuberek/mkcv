# Instructional Designer

**Company:** Northpine Outfitters
**Team:** Learning and Development
**Location:** Remote (US, Eastern or Central time zones preferred)
**Employment type:** Full time
**Salary range:** $68,000 to $82,000

## About Northpine Outfitters

Northpine Outfitters sells outdoor gear and apparel through 180 stores and
three distribution centers. Our 9,000 associates range from seasonal store
staff to forklift operators to store managers, and many of them learn on a
phone during a shift. The Learning and Development team builds the training
that gets them confident fast and keeps them safe.

## About the role

You will design and build training for store and distribution center teams,
from first-week onboarding to safety and leadership programs. You will own
projects end to end: needs analysis, design, development, launch in our LMS,
and evaluation. We are looking for someone who writes clearly, builds clean
e-learning, and asks "what should people do differently?" before opening an
authoring tool.

## What you will do

- Conduct needs analysis with operations leaders, store managers, and frontline associates
- Design learning solutions using ADDIE, choosing the right mix of e-learning, job aids, and instructor-led sessions
- Write storyboards and design documents and run review cycles with subject matter experts
- Build interactive e-learning in Articulate Storyline and Rise, including scenarios and knowledge checks
- Publish, enroll, and maintain courses in our LMS and report on completion
- Evaluate programs with the Kirkpatrick model and revise courses based on results
- Create quick-reference guides and short videos for store teams
- Facilitate train-the-trainer sessions for district trainers

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Two or more years of instructional design experience, in a corporate setting or a related field such as teaching
- Portfolio that shows e-learning built in Articulate Storyline or Rise
- Experience applying ADDIE or a similar instructional design model
- Experience working with subject matter experts to turn complex content into clear learning
- Strong writing and editing skills
- Comfort managing several projects at once with clear timelines

### Preferred

- Experience designing for frontline, hourly, or deskless workers
- Experience with Workday Learning or another enterprise LMS
- Camtasia or other video editing experience
- Experience with safety or compliance training
- Accessibility knowledge for e-learning

## How we work

- A small team of five designers who review each other's work every week
- We visit stores and distribution centers before we design for them
- We measure whether training changed performance, not only whether it was completed
