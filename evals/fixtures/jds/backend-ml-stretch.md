# Senior ML Platform Engineer

**Company:** Cyberdyne Systems
**Team:** Machine Learning Platform
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Cyberdyne Systems

Cyberdyne Systems builds forecasting software for logistics companies. Our
data scientists train and ship models that predict demand and delivery times.
The Machine Learning Platform team gives them the infrastructure to do it
safely and repeatably.

## About the role

You will build the platform that data scientists use to move from notebooks to
production. The work spans Kubernetes, backend services in Go, and the
tooling around the model lifecycle. You will work closely with research
scientists who care about reproducibility and speed.

## What you will do

- Build and operate the services that run model training jobs and batch inference on Kubernetes
- Own the model registry and experiment tracking, built on MLflow
- Support PyTorch workloads, including GPU scheduling, container images, and dataset access
- Write backend services and tooling in Go for model deployment and rollback
- Design observability for model serving: latency, throughput, errors, and drift signals
- Automate model training pipelines and make runs reproducible
- Work with data scientists on performance profiling and cost control
- Participate in the on-call rotation for the platform

## What we are looking for

### Required

- At least five years of backend or infrastructure engineering
- Strong experience running workloads on Kubernetes
- Professional experience writing Go
- Experience supporting machine learning teams, including model training and deployment
- Working knowledge of PyTorch and how its jobs behave on shared clusters
- Experience operating PostgreSQL or a similar data store

### Preferred

- Experience with MLflow or a comparable experiment tracking tool
- Experience with GPU scheduling and autoscaling
- Experience reducing cloud costs for compute-heavy workloads
- Familiarity with feature stores and data versioning

## How we work

- Data scientists are our customers, and we ask them what slows them down
- Short feedback loops: weekly demos of platform changes
- Written design documents for changes that touch the training pipeline
- Reproducibility is a requirement, not a nice-to-have

## Benefits

- Generous parental leave and flexible time off
