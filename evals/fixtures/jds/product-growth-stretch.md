# Growth Product Manager

**Company:** Acme Corp
**Team:** Growth
**Location:** Remote (US)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Acme Corp

Acme Corp sells scheduling software to small field service businesses. Most
customers start with a free trial, so the first weeks decide whether they stay.
The Growth team owns that journey from signup to the first successful week.

## About the role

You will be the product manager for activation and retention. You will find
where trial users drop out, form hypotheses, run experiments, and ship the
changes that move the numbers. This is a metrics-driven role, and you will
spend a lot of time with data.

## What you will do

- Own the activation and retention roadmap for the free trial and early customer lifecycle
- Analyze funnel data with SQL to find where users drop off and why
- Design and run experiments on our A/B testing platform, from hypothesis to readout
- Use Amplitude to build funnels, cohorts, and retention reports for the team
- Set metric goals with engineering and design, and review results every week
- Write experiment briefs and product requirement documents
- Work with lifecycle marketing on onboarding emails and in-product messages
- Share learnings with the wider company in a monthly growth review

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Three or more years of product management in a growth, activation, or experimentation role
- Strong SQL skills and comfort answering your own data questions
- Hands-on experience running experiments on an A/B testing platform
- Experience with Amplitude or a similar product analytics tool
- Solid understanding of statistical significance, sample size, and experiment design
- Excellent written communication

### Preferred

- Experience with self-serve onboarding and free trial conversion
- Familiarity with lifecycle marketing and pricing experiments
- A technical background or past work closely with engineers
- Experience building growth models in a spreadsheet

## How we work

- Hypotheses and results are written down, including the experiments that fail
- Each team reviews its funnel every week
- Small experiments before large rebuilds
- Product managers query data themselves instead of waiting for reports

## Benefits

- Competitive salary and equity
- Remote-first with a yearly in-person team week
- Home office stipend and learning budget
- Generous parental leave and flexible time off

## Interview process

1. A conversation with the hiring manager
2. A discussion of an experiment you ran and what you learned
3. A data exercise using a funnel table and SQL
4. Conversations with an engineer, a designer, and a lifecycle marketer
