# Accounting Manager

**Company:** Bramwell Industrial Supply
**Department:** Finance and Accounting
**Location:** Ardenvale, MN (on site four days a week)
**Employment type:** Full time, exempt
**Salary range:** $105,000 to $125,000 plus annual bonus

## About Bramwell Industrial Supply

Bramwell Industrial Supply is a privately held distributor of industrial and
safety products with $210M in annual revenue, 11 branches, and a growing
e-commerce channel. We are family owned and have been profitable for 40 years.
Our finance team of 12 reports to the Controller and CFO.

## Position summary

The Accounting Manager runs our month-end close, leads a team of three
accountants, and owns the general ledger. You will keep our financial
statements accurate and on time under GAAP, prepare for the annual audit, and
improve how we close the books. This role reports to the Controller and is a
path to Assistant Controller.

## Key responsibilities

- Manage the month-end and year-end close and keep the close calendar on schedule (currently 8 business days; goal of 5)
- Review journal entries, account reconciliations, and balance sheet schedules prepared by the team
- Prepare monthly financial statements and flux analysis for the Controller and CFO
- Own revenue recognition under ASC 606, including rebates and distributor incentive programs
- Manage inventory accounting with operations, including cycle count results and reserves
- Coordinate the annual financial statement audit and prepare audit schedules
- Supervise, train, and develop two staff accountants and one AP specialist
- Improve close processes and automate reconciliations in NetSuite and Excel
- Partner with branch managers on accruals, freight costs, and inventory adjustments

## Qualifications

### Required

- Bachelor's degree in accounting
- CPA license
- Six or more years of progressive accounting experience, including public accounting or a controller-led private company
- At least two years supervising accountants
- Strong knowledge of GAAP, including ASC 606
- Experience leading a month-end close
- NetSuite experience
- Advanced Excel skills

### Preferred

- Experience in distribution or manufacturing
- Experience with FloQast or another close management tool
- ERP implementation experience
- Experience adopting ASC 842 lease accounting
