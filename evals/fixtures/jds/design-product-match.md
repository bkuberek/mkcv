# Senior Product Designer

**Company:** Corbel Workflow
**Team:** Product Design
**Location:** Remote (US and Canada)
**Employment type:** Full time
**Salary range:** $150,000 to $178,000 plus equity

## About Corbel Workflow

Corbel Workflow is B2B SaaS for operations teams in regulated industries:
clinics, insurers, and lenders. Teams use Corbel to route work, track
deadlines, and prove compliance. Our customers spend hours a day in the
product, so small usability problems turn into lost days. We have 220
employees and a design team of nine.

## About the role

You will be the senior product designer for our work queue and case
management experience. You will work with a product manager and two
engineering squads, own design from discovery to delivery, and contribute to
our design system. We are looking for someone who has shipped complex,
data-dense workflows and can show how research changed the design.

## What you will do

- Lead end-to-end design for the work queue and case detail views, from discovery to launch
- Plan and run user research with operations staff, including interviews and contextual inquiry
- Run usability testing on prototypes and live features, and share findings with the team
- Build prototyping flows in Figma that let engineers and customers react early
- Contribute components, patterns, and documentation to our design systems work
- Make accessibility part of every design and help us meet WCAG 2.1 AA
- Work with product managers on scope and trade-offs, and with engineers through delivery
- Present work to leadership and customers

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Six or more years of product design experience, including at least three on B2B SaaS products
- A portfolio that shows complex workflows, your process, and measurable outcomes
- Expert-level Figma skills, including components, variables, and prototyping
- Experience planning and running user research and usability testing
- Experience building or contributing to design systems
- Working knowledge of WCAG and accessible design patterns
- Strong written communication for design rationale

### Preferred

- Experience in healthcare, insurance, or financial services software
- Experience with product analytics tools such as Amplitude or Mixpanel
- Experience designing data visualizations and dashboards
- Comfort reading HTML and CSS

## How we work

- Designers are embedded in squads and attend planning and demos
- We run a weekly design critique and a monthly research share-out
- We prefer small releases and fast feedback over big launches

## Benefits

- Medical, dental, and vision coverage
- Equity for every employee
- Flexible time off with a minimum of 15 days
- A yearly learning and conference budget

## Interview process

1. Recruiter call
2. Portfolio presentation to the design team
3. A collaborative design exercise with a product manager
4. Conversations with an engineer and the head of design
