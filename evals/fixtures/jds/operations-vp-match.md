# Vice President, Operations and Supply Chain

**Company:** Hearthline Brands
**Reports to:** Chief Executive Officer
**Location:** Fennick, TX (on site, with regular travel to distribution centers)
**Employment type:** Full time, executive

## About Hearthline Brands

Hearthline Brands owns three consumer home brands (cookware, small appliances,
and outdoor furniture) with $520M in annual revenue. About 55% of sales come
from big-box and specialty retail partners and 45% from our own e-commerce
sites. We import most products from Asia and operate three distribution
centers, one of them run by a 3PL. The company is backed by a growth equity
firm and plans to reach $800M in revenue within four years.

## The opportunity

Our next VP of Operations and Supply Chain will lead planning, sourcing
logistics, distribution, transportation, and customer fulfillment, with an
organization of about 900 people. Retail partners have told us our OTIF
performance must improve, and our network was built for a smaller company.
This is a hands-on executive role for someone who has scaled a multi-channel
distribution network and built the team to run it.

## Responsibilities

- Own the operations and supply chain P&L and an operating budget of about $80M
- Lead a monthly S&OP process with sales, marketing, product, and finance
- Raise OTIF to retail partners from 89% to at least 95% and reduce chargebacks
- Improve inventory turns and working capital without hurting in-stock rates
- Redesign the distribution network for growth, including a possible fourth DC and 3PL partnerships
- Lead the selection and rollout of a new WMS across our owned DCs
- Manage carrier and 3PL relationships and contracts for parcel, LTL, truckload, and ocean freight
- Build a Lean culture of daily management and continuous improvement
- Keep safety as the first metric in every operations review
- Develop the leadership team and build succession plans

## Qualifications

### Required

- 15 or more years in supply chain and distribution operations, including at least 5 at the vice president or senior director level
- Experience leading multi-site distribution operations for both retail wholesale and direct-to-consumer channels
- Track record of measurable improvement in OTIF, cost per unit, and inventory turns
- P&L ownership and experience presenting to a CEO and board
- Experience implementing a WMS
- Deep experience with Lean or Six Sigma
- Bachelor's degree in supply chain, engineering, or business

### Preferred

- MBA
- Experience in home goods, housewares, or consumer durables
- Experience with SAP S/4HANA or a similar ERP migration
- Import logistics experience, including port strategy
- APICS certification
