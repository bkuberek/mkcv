# Marketing Coordinator (Entry Level)

**Company:** Bluefinch Coffee Roasters
**Team:** Marketing
**Location:** Larkin Falls, GA (hybrid, three days in our roastery office)
**Employment type:** Full time
**Pay:** $46,000 to $52,000 per year

## About Bluefinch Coffee Roasters

Bluefinch is a specialty coffee roaster with 6 cafes, a wholesale business
that supplies 120 restaurants and grocers, and an online subscription that
ships to 8,000 customers each month. We are a small, friendly team that cares
about good coffee, fair sourcing, and telling the stories of the farms we buy
from.

## About the role

Our Marketing Coordinator keeps our channels running and sounding like us.
You will plan and publish our social media, write our weekly emails, make
graphics and short videos, and help run events at our cafes. This is an
entry-level role: we will teach you a lot, and we want someone who already
loves making content.

## What you will do

- Plan and maintain the content calendar for Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook
- Shoot and edit short videos and photos at the cafes and the roastery
- Create graphics in Canva for social media, the website, and in-store signage
- Write and send our weekly subscriber email and seasonal campaigns in Mailchimp
- Do copywriting for product pages, coffee descriptions, and menu boards
- Pull monthly reports from Google Analytics and social platforms and share what worked
- Coordinate cafe events, tastings, and pop-ups with the cafe managers
- Respond to comments and messages on our social media accounts

## What we are looking for

### Required

- A bachelor's degree in marketing, communications, or a related field, or equivalent experience
- Experience running social media for a brand, a business, a club, or a campus organization
- A portfolio of content you made: posts, videos, emails, or writing samples
- Strong copywriting and editing skills
- Comfort with Canva and a video editing app
- Organized enough to keep a content calendar on track every week

### Preferred

- An internship or part-time job in marketing
- Experience with Mailchimp or Klaviyo
- Basic Google Analytics knowledge
- Photography skills and experience with Photoshop or Lightroom
- Experience in food, beverage, or hospitality

## Why you will like it here

- Free coffee, obviously, plus a monthly bag to take home
- A manager who will give you real projects and real feedback
- Health, dental, and vision insurance and a 401(k)
- Paid time off and a paid volunteer day

## How to apply

Send your resume and a link to two or three pieces of content you are proud
of. Tell us your favorite coffee drink, even if it is iced and very sweet.

Bluefinch Coffee Roasters is an equal opportunity employer.
