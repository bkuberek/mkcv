# MLOps Engineer, Data Science

**Company:** Cyberdyne Systems
**Team:** Data Science Platform
**Location:** Boston, MA (hybrid)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Cyberdyne Systems

Cyberdyne Systems builds forecasting software for logistics companies. Our
data scientists build models that predict demand and delivery times, and the
Data Science Platform team keeps those models running reliably in production.

## About the role

We are looking for an MLOps engineer who can take a data scientist's analysis
and make it a dependable production service. You will build the pipelines that
retrain and deploy models, monitor them after launch, and help data scientists
adopt better engineering habits. You will be a team lead for projects that
span several models and mentor junior colleagues.

## What you will do

- Build and schedule model training and batch scoring pipelines with Airflow
- Package models as containers and deploy them to Kubernetes
- Implement model monitoring for data drift, prediction quality, and latency
- Maintain feature pipelines and keep data definitions consistent between training and serving
- Act as team lead on cross-team MLOps projects, planning work and reviewing designs
- Write Python libraries that make common data science tasks repeatable
- Work with data scientists on testing, versioning, and code review practices
- Respond to production incidents involving models and data pipelines

## What we are looking for

### Required

- Three or more years in MLOps, data engineering, or backend engineering
- Strong Python skills
- Experience with workflow orchestration, such as Airflow
- Experience deploying services to Kubernetes
- Experience with model monitoring and alerting
- Ability to act as team lead or technical lead on projects

### Preferred

- Experience with feature stores and experiment tracking
- Experience with cloud data warehouses and SQL
- Experience reducing the cost of training and serving
- Mentoring or teaching experience

## How we work

- Models are production software: they have tests, owners, and on-call coverage
- Data scientists and platform engineers pair on the first deployment
- Written design documents for changes to shared pipelines
- Blameless reviews after incidents

## Benefits

- Competitive salary and equity
- Hybrid schedule with a commuter benefit
- Conference and learning budget
- Generous parental leave and flexible time off

## Interview process

1. A conversation with the hiring manager
2. A technical discussion about a pipeline you have run in production
3. A design exercise on retraining and monitoring a model
4. Conversations with a data scientist and a platform engineer
