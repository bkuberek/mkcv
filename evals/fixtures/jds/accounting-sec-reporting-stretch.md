# Senior Manager, SEC Reporting and Technical Accounting

**Company:** Solvane Therapeutics, Inc.
**Department:** Corporate Controller's Group
**Location:** Ardenvale, MN (hybrid, three days on site)
**Employment type:** Full time

## About Solvane Therapeutics

Solvane Therapeutics is a publicly traded, commercial-stage biotechnology
company with two approved products and a pipeline of five clinical programs.
We have operations in the US and a subsidiary in Ireland that reports under
IFRS for statutory purposes. Our finance organization has 45 people.

## About the role

You will lead our external reporting. You will own the preparation of our
Form 10-K and Form 10-Q filings, our earnings release tables, and the XBRL
tagging that goes with them. You will also write technical accounting memos
for complex transactions such as collaboration agreements, royalty
arrangements, and convertible debt, and you will be a key owner of our SOX
compliance program.

## Responsibilities

- Own the SEC reporting calendar and prepare the Form 10-K, Form 10-Q, and Form 8-K financial disclosures
- Manage XBRL tagging and review with our filing agent
- Draft technical accounting memos under US GAAP for collaboration revenue, ASC 606 milestone payments, leases, and debt
- Partner with the external audit team on quarterly reviews and the annual integrated audit
- Maintain SOX 404 controls over financial reporting and disclosure, and remediate deficiencies
- Monitor new accounting standards and SEC rules and assess their impact
- Prepare the disclosure committee materials and support the audit committee
- Coach two senior accountants on research and documentation

## Qualifications

### Required

- CPA license
- Eight or more years of experience, including at least three in public accounting with public company audit clients
- At least three years of hands-on SEC reporting experience, including drafting the Form 10-K
- Experience with SOX 404 compliance at a public company
- Strong US GAAP research and technical accounting memo writing
- Experience reviewing XBRL tagging

### Preferred

- Life sciences or biotechnology industry experience
- Knowledge of IFRS for foreign subsidiaries
- Experience with collaboration and licensing arrangements under ASC 606 and ASC 808
- Workiva experience
